HOW TO PLAY ON ANY PC:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: (FIRST TIME ONLY ON A NEW PC)
  Run "Install-Driver.bat" as Administrator.
  (Or if Controller.exe is already open, simply click the yellow 
   "INSTALL GAMEPAD DRIVER" button on the top right!)
  This installs the official ViGEmBus driver so Windows recognizes
  your smartphone as genuine Xbox 360 controllers.

STEP 2: LAUNCH CONTROLLER
  Double-click "Controller.exe".
  The Tactical Telemetry HUD opens immediately with live oscilloscopes!

STEP 3: CONNECT YOUR PHONE
  - Connect your smartphone to the same Wi-Fi network as this PC.
  - Open your phone's camera and scan the QR Code shown in the GUI.
  - If a browser security warning appears, tap "Advanced" -> "Proceed"
    (this is normal because the server generates local SSL certificates).
  - Rotate your phone to Landscape mode.
  - Done! Player 1 lights up in Neon Cyan on your PC screen!

STEP 4: CONTROLLER CONTROLS & ERGONOMICS
  - Left Side: Adaptive Thumbstick / D-Pad
    * Quick Flicks (<160ms): Adaptive discrete impulse with +/-16° cardinal locking for 
      crisp 1-step menu navigation in FIFA, Rocket League, and Steam menus (no over-scrolling!).
    * Sustained Drag (>=160ms): Full 360-degree continuous analog movement for driving and sprinting.
    * Tactical D-Pad: Tap [MODE: DPAD] on screen to switch to a classic discrete 4-Way D-Pad.
  - Triggers & Bumpers: Pure Esports Hair-Triggers
    * LT & RT: Instantaneous 100% hair-trigger actuation (XInput 255) on touch, strictly dropping 
      to 0.0 with redundant zero-state burst upon release. No floating or stuck trigger values!
    * LB & RB: High-speed shoulder bumpers.
  - Right Side: Y, X, B, A Diamond Action Buttons
  - Center of YAXB: Interactive Right Analog Thumbstick!
    * Touch & drag to control camera / look around in GTA, Cyberpunk, shooters, or skill moves in FIFA!
    * Quick tap acts as RS (Right Stick thumb click)!

STEP 5: PLAYER SLOT SWAPPING (FIFA & MULTIPLAYER FIX)
  - If FIFA or another game assigns players in the wrong order:
    * Tap the [⇄ SWAP] button on the Player Deck card in the PC GUI, or
    * Click "⇄ SWAP P1 / P2" in the left sidebar.
  - The slot manager atomically exchanges the two virtual XInput controllers,
    instantly swapping Player 1 and Player 2 without disconnecting anyone!

STEP 6: LEGENDARY REAL FORCE-FEEDBACK HAPTICS
  - Native dual-motor rumble feedback is extracted directly from the game
    via ViGEmBus kernel notifications (crashes, tackles, ball kicks, engine revs, goal posts).
  - Streamed to your phone at ultra-low latency with:
    * Dual-tier multi-frequency physical motor pulses.
    * Sub-bass acoustic tactile resonance (50Hz-70Hz) via the phone's speaker.
  - TIP: For maximum haptic intensity, ensure your phone is NOT in silent/mute mode.

STEP 7: TESTING WITH ONLINE TESTERS OR ANY PC GAME
  - Online Tester: Open https://hardwaretester.com/gamepad or https://gamepad-tester.com
    In the Controller GUI, click [TEST] on P1, or tap any button on your phone.
    The browser will immediately detect "Xbox 360 Controller (XInput STANDARD GAMEPAD)"!
  - In Games: Launch any PC game (FIFA, Rocket League, GTA V, Elden Ring, Forza, etc.).
    The controller is recognized natively as genuine Xbox 360 gamepads!

SUPPORT:
  Supports up to 4 simultaneous smartphones (Player 1, 2, 3, 4)
  for local multiplayer split-screen gaming with dedicated live telemetry!
